Building Inspection Report

August 16, 2013 · PDF · 81 pages

Prepared by Brenda Bello, RA and Ari Kamo, PE.

Tenants commissioned this report as part of their fight to protect rent stabilization and affordable housing. The authors concluded that the building did not meet the substantial rehabilitation criteria for an exemption from rent stabilization.

Only two of 17 system categories met the criteria; 14 failed and one did not apply. The report documented sewage backups, water damage, fire safety hazards, incomplete renovations, and accessibility failures, supporting tenants’ demands for rent protections and safe, habitable homes.

More than 13 years later, nearly all of these issues persist. Ownership has allowed the building to deteriorate even further, while raising rents ever higher.

Letter to Management

October 31, 2026 · PDF · 1 page

This short letter notifies ownership that MLFTA has re-formed and requests a meeting with tenant representatives. It outlines several shared concerns.

It does not catalogue the many problems in individual units and throughout the building: persistent water leaks, mold growth, rodent and other pest infestations, continual sewage backups, and a lack of heat, hot water, and basic building safety measures.
